Live online 1-on-1 coaching

A private yoga teacher on your screen, watching as you move

A pre-recorded video can't see that your knee has drifted past your ankle. Your James Yoga coach can, and tells you straight away.

  1. Pick your coach and time

    Filter by style, language and speciality (back pain, pregnancy, runners, total beginners). Slots run from 06:00 to 22:30, seven days a week.

  2. Do a 5-minute movement check

    Your coach watches a few simple movements to see how you move and where you're tight, then builds the session around you.

  3. Practise with live cues

    Prop your phone or laptop up where it can see your mat. Your coach cues, corrects and adapts in real time over two-way video.

  4. Take home your plan

    After each session you get a written practice plan and a private replay, so you can repeat it on your own between sessions.

Our story

It started on the Night Tube, at four in the morning

James never planned to open a yoga studio. He drove the Northern line through the night, and after two years of 3am shifts in a driver's cab his lower back had given up on him.

"I thought yoga was for people with free time. It turned out to be for people with none."James, founder
  1. 2016

    The 4am Club

    On his breaks in the Morden depot canteen, James starts stretching between the tables. Within a month six other drivers join him, and the cleaners call them "the 4am Club."

  2. 2017

    Teacher training on days off

    He spends his rest days on 200 hours of yoga teacher training and practises cueing poses on anyone who will stand still, mostly his flatmate.

  3. 2018

    A railway arch in Bermondsey

    The first studio opens under a railway arch. A train rumbles overhead every few minutes, so James turns it into a practice: hold the pose and breathe until it passes. Rumble Breath becomes our best-loved class.

  4. 2020

    Gran Maggie and the ironing board

    In lockdown, James's 84-year-old gran in Aberdeen asks for help with her hip. He props a laptop on the ironing board and coaches her live, twice a week. Her friends ask for sessions too, and live 1-on-1 online coaching is born. 1,000 sessions are booked in the first month.

  5. 2022

    North of the river, then north of the border

    Studios open in a former cotton mill in Manchester's Ancoats and a converted warehouse in Glasgow's Finnieston. Members start asking whether one membership could cover both.

  6. 2024

    The Studio Passport

    It can. One membership now covers every city, with a free Rain Check swap to online whenever the weather wins.

  7. 2026

    13 cities and one very loyal member

    Today 60+ coaches teach in 13 cities and online. There's still no studio in Aberdeen. Maggie is 90 and still books every Tuesday at 10:00.

Questions

Before you roll out your mat

Is my first class really free?

Yes. Your first studio class in any of our 13 cities is free, and your first live online 1-on-1 taster (20 minutes) is free too. No card needed to book.

How does live online 1-on-1 yoga coaching work?

You pick a coach and a time, join a two-way video call from your phone, tablet or laptop, and your coach watches and corrects your alignment as you move. Afterwards you get a written plan and a replay. All you need is enough floor space for a mat and a device that can see you.

Can I use one membership in different cities?

Yes. The Studio Passport covers unlimited classes at every James Yoga studio in the UK, plus Rain Check swaps to online sessions.

I've never done yoga. Is that a problem?

Not at all. Around a third of our members start with no experience. Beginner-labelled classes run daily in every city, and a 1-on-1 session is a good way to start if you'd rather learn in private.

Can I cancel my membership?

Yes. The Studio Passport is rolling monthly with no minimum term. Cancel from your account before your next billing date.

Do you teach people with injuries or during pregnancy?

Many of our coaches hold additional training in pregnancy and injury-aware yoga. Tell us when you book and we'll match you with the right coach. Please check with your GP or midwife first.
